Three directions for the new crosswaysa.org

All three keep the CW mark, navy and amber, and the current site's content. Each mockup below uses the live site's service times, address, latest sermon, upcoming events and Three Steps. Items marked "to confirm" are placeholders. Striped areas show where the church's own photos go. Pick one and I'll narrow it into the final design.

Idea

San Antonio's limestone arches at blue hour. Each arch is a lit doorway holding service times, the headline and the latest message, and they light up one by one. The same arch shape frames photos and the three steps.

Look

NavyAmberLimestoneMist

Young Serif for headings, Figtree for text.

Motion

On load the arches light from the edges to the centre. Further down, each step's doorway fills with light as you reach it. Nothing loops.

Risk

The heaviest first impression, so it needs real photography to balance the dark navy opening. Arches can recall Spanish missions, so only the shape is used, with no religious iconography.

Idea

The site works like good airport signage. A ruled grid, one route from the parking lot to the sanctuary, and sign bars that take members to Live, Sermons, Events and Give in one click. The Three Steps become stops on a line.

Look

NavyAmberGroundRule

Schibsted Grotesk for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for text. Atkinson was designed for low-vision readers.

Motion

The route line draws itself from the first stop to the last on load. The Three Steps line fills as you scroll. Event rows step in once.

Risk

The clearest and most institutional of the three. Warmth has to come from the photos and the writing. The route stops shown are placeholders until the church confirms its Sunday flow.

Idea

A cut-paper landscape of Texas hills with a lit church door, built from flat layers with soft shadows. The layers return in the steps and the section edges, so the page feels assembled rather than stacked.

Look

NavyAmberSteelMistSage

Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Public Sans for text.

Motion

On load the paper layers settle into place from back to front. The hills drift at slightly different speeds as you scroll. The three steps paste in one after another.

Risk

The warmest and most illustrated. It can read as folksy unless the drawing is excellent, so the final build needs a custom illustration set rather than stock shapes.

Scroll inside each mockup to see the page motion. Motion is off when your system asks for reduced motion.
