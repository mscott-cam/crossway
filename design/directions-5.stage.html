Three concepts in the spirit of Table Fellowship, with the Crossway logo

These follow the reference more closely: a warm, confident layout with big type, a block menu button, photo blocks that grow as you scroll, and scripture set large in italic. The logo is used directly throughout. I traced it into a vector so the cross mark and the lettering can be used together or apart, in any colour. Items marked "to confirm" are placeholders, and flat blue panels show where the church's own photos go.

Idea

The closest to the reference. A warm paper ground, an amber announcement bar and block menu button, and the cross mark above a single wide word that changes between Connect, Consider and Commit. A photo block grows to full width as you scroll, and scripture is set large in italic.

Look

PaperNavyAmberMist

Archivo Expanded capitals, Instrument Serif Italic, Hanken Grotesk for reading.

Motion

The word swaps every few seconds. The photo block widens as you scroll and the scripture lights up word by word. The menu opens as a panel.

Risk

Paper with a serif italic is a familiar pairing, so the cross mark and amber have to carry the identity. It only feels alive with real photographs.

Idea

The same ideas after dark. Deep navy with the logo in its original amber and white, a giant headline, the cross mark cropped large at the edge, a slow strip of photos, and the three steps as cards that stack as you scroll.

Look

NightAmberCreamSlate

Archivo Expanded capitals, Instrument Serif Italic, Hanken Grotesk for reading.

Motion

The headline lifts in line by line, the photo strip drifts slowly, step cards stack and settle as you scroll, and the wordmark rises into the footer.

Risk

Dark pages are harder for some older readers, so body text stays large and high contrast. The photos have to be bright enough to carry the page.

Idea

Amber up front. The hero is the site's table of contents in big type: plan a visit, watch, groups, kids, events, give. It serves a newcomer and a member in one glance. Below, the page moves in solid colour blocks.

Look

AmberNavyPaperMist

Archivo Expanded capitals, Instrument Serif Italic, Hanken Grotesk for reading.

Motion

The headline and directory rows slide in, rows invert on hover, and the colour blocks reveal as you scroll.

Risk

The loudest of the three. Large amber areas need restraint to stay serious, and the directory needs the church to agree on its top six destinations.
