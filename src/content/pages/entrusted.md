## Understanding ENTRUSTED

ENTRUSTED _is a 2-year, church-wide initiative focused on discipleship, generosity, and the unique opportunity and potential God has given us to steward for His glory._

### Main Goal

<p class="entrusted-figure">100% Engagement</p>

The main goal of ENTRUSTED is for **every person and every household** at Crossway to be transformed by fresh devotion, prayer, and studying what God says about faithful, joyful stewardship.

### Financial Target

<p class="entrusted-figure">$6,500,000</p>

The ENTRUSTED initiative is also targeting an increase in total giving over two years to fund faithful development of the extraordinary opportunities God has entrusted to Crossway.

## One Fund. Two Years. Three Aims.

This is a **one-fund initiative**, so the amount includes our

1. **Existing** ministry fund for the **next two years**,
2. **Expanding** the useable space God has given us, and
3. **Extending** ministry approaches into more classroom teaching, as well as partnerships with church planters, local ministries, and international work.

### One Fund?

You may have been part of church campaigns in the past where you were asked to give a second gift “above and beyond” your usual gifts to complete special projects. This type of giving is potentially confusing to people who don’t have a baseline for their “usual giving.” We believe all our efforts are on ONE MISSION—to make disciples.

### Two Years?

The Entrusted initiative will last two years - from December 2026 to December 2028. In those two years, every gift given to Crossway will go to the Entrusted initiative—for **Existing** disciple-making, **Expanding** disciple-making, and **Extending** disciple-making.

## Aim 1

### Existing ministry

_Our first aim in this initiative is to continue funding our existing ministry._ Currently over 600 people attend Crossway each Sunday! Crossway members are in small groups practicing mutual ministry care for one another, which is also a training ground for developing disciple-making leaders. Ministry teams are staffed and led by beautiful servants of the Lord. Some core studies are in place to further equip the saints. Crossway Kids and Students have grown year-over-year and work to help families make disciples. It’s astonishing to think of all the ministry that is accomplished week after week in this church body!

## Aim 2

### Expanding ministry

_Our second aim in this initiative is to expand our useable space outdoors and upstairs_. We’re so thankful for the lower floor we fill each weekend and use throughout the week. God knew what we needed and worked through a pandemic to make this space available at a price we could afford. Yet above the lobby sits an entire second floor unused—a “talent” essentially buried! And even at full capacity, we don’t use all the parking God has given us. Our outdoor space could better serve our church, especially our many young families. Imagine upstairs offices, space to train preachers, planters, and counselors, Christian education, Student Ministry, and fellowship gatherings. Picture a covered playground, passenger drop-off, and beautified landscaping. God has entrusted us with much, for more.

## Aim 3

### Extending ministry

Our third aim in this initiative is to extend our ministry approaches and partnerships. More usable space and greater generosity at Crossway will help us respond to needs and pursue new ministry opportunities. That includes extended teaching in theology, Christian living, counseling, Bible study, and more, complementing the discipleship already happening in our small groups. We also hope to deepen partnerships, especially in international church planting and missions, and to share our space for broader community and kingdom impact—from pastoral training organizations to mercy ministries and Christian education. God is extending our opportunities through ENTRUSTED!
