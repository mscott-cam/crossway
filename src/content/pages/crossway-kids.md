> We will not hide them from their children, but tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD, and his might, and the wonders that he has done. He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers to teach to their children, _**that the next generation might know them**_, the children yet unborn, and arise and tell them to their children, so that they should set their hope in God and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments.

## Same Mission

Every Sunday we have trained & screened volunteers that serve our little ones. From the games, to the singing, to the lessons, and activities — our aim is to come alongside parents in making little disciples of Christ!
