We partner with parents in raising the next generation to worship Christ, walk with Christ, and work for Christ. We want to help students understand the Word of God and how it applies to their lives.

Our ministry time includes worship in song, teaching from the Bible, time for personal application, and games! We spend time in gender- and age-specific small groups led by adult leaders, applying God’s word together and growing in an authentic community.

## Gathering Schedule

During the school year, we meet from 3-5 pm on the 2nd, 3rd and 4th Sundays.

During the summer, we meet on the 2nd & 4th Sundays.
