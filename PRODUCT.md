# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed on Vercel (confirmed by user).

## Users

- Primary: first-time visitors to Crossway Bible Church in San Antonio, TX, across all ages, deciding whether and how to attend a first Sunday.
- Secondary: members and regular attendees who need fast access to sermons, the Sunday livestream, events, and giving.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Crossway Bible Church (15759 San Pedro Ave, San Antonio, TX 78232), replacing crosswaysa.org (Webflow). It helps a newcomer plan a first visit and take the next step, and gives the congregation one place for sermons, live, events, ministries, and giving. Success is a visitor who knows when to arrive, what to expect, and how to connect.

## Positioning

A church that meets Sundays at 9:00 and 10:45 and exists "to glorify God through the fulfillment of the Great Commission", with expository preaching (Bold Preaching, Passionate Worship, Purposeful Discipleship, Fervent Prayer, Courageous Evangelism, Church Planting) and a three-step path: Connect, Consider, Commit.

## Operating Context

- Sunday services at 9:00 and 10:45; over 600 attend on Sundays.
- Sermons archive of roughly 600 messages (about 103 pages), organized by series, speaker, and Bible book, with a featured latest message.
- Events and registration run through Church Center; giving through Pushpay; bookstore through 10ofthose; live stream at live.crosswaysa.org.
- Monthly Prayer & Praise on the first Wednesday.
- ENTRUSTED: a two-year stewardship initiative (Dec 2026 to Dec 2028), commitment begins Sunday Dec 13, 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all existing sections: About (Leadership, Beliefs, Child Safety, Networks), Church Life (Small Groups, Students, Kids, Biblical Counseling), Resources (Sermons, Sunday Live, Bookstore), Events, Give, Español, and the Questions / Prayer form.
- Kids ministry age groups: Infants, Walkers, 2's-3's, Pre-K/Kinder, 1st-2nd Grade, 3rd-5th Grade.
- Open facts the church must supply: parking and arrival details, kids check-in process, child-safety policy text, belief statements, form backend, whether a CMS is needed, whether the Español site is folded in.
- Old Webflow URLs must redirect.

## Brand Commitments

- Church name: Crossway Bible Church.
- Keep the CW logo and the navy (#1a374d) and amber (#f5a707) colors.
- Voice: warm, welcoming, direct, with short headlines.
- Requested qualities: restraint, clarity, focused; not too casual but approachable; warm and appealing to every age.
- Motion: GSAP, subtle and helpful for page flow and interaction, never over the top.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real church photography exists but has not been provided yet; mockups use labeled placeholders.
- Real copy from crosswaysa.org: mission, six commitments with verses, three steps, leadership roster, ENTRUSTED details, recent sermons, upcoming events.
- No testimonials, history, or leader bios exist on the current site; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The first-time visitor's path comes first; members get one-tap shortcuts.
2. Say the practical things plainly: when, where, what to expect, what about my kids.
3. Restraint over decoration; every element earns its place.
4. Motion clarifies flow and never competes with reading.
5. Never fabricate church facts; mark unknowns as placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable by all ages: generous type sizes, strong contrast (WCAG AA or better), large touch targets, full keyboard support, and reduced-motion support.
